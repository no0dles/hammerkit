## Fixed
- **Env references in service volume names.** A volume such as
  `${DATA_PREFIX}-db:/data` resolves `$NAME` and `${NAME}` from the service's envs
  before it is split into name and path, so a volume can be named per CI job on a
  shared docker host, and a value containing a colon no longer breaks the split.
